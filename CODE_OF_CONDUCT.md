# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be kind and patient with people at every career stage, and criticise
ideas and code, never people. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer (@msc92) privately through GitHub. Reports are kept
confidential.
